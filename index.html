The organizer’s brief

NEXUS Invitational is renting a converted warehouse for a Saturday championship. Setup is Friday. Doors open Saturday at 5:30 p.m.; the final begins at 7.

Protect equipment, player information, tournament systems, and the livestream while keeping the event moving. Emergency exits must stay usable; do not alter fire-safety systems.
